# Digital Arrest Kya Hai?

Digital Arrest ek scam hai jahan fraudster khud ko police, telecom officer, ya cyber crime official batate hain aur victim par turant paise bhejne ka pressure banate hain.

## Scam Ka Common Pattern

1. Aapko threatening call aata hai ki aapka number ya Aadhaar kisi crime mein use hua hai.
2. Caller urgency create karta hai aur bolta hai ki aap investigation mein ho.
3. Video call par rehne ko bolta hai aur family se baat karne se rokta hai.
4. "Verification payment" ke naam par paise transfer karwata hai.

## Red Flags

- Phone call par turant arrest ki dhamki
- OTP, PIN, screen share, ya instant transfer ki demand
- Fake legal language aur spoofed caller IDs

## Kya Karna Chahiye

- Call turant cut karo.
- Official helpline ya local police se verify karo.
- cybercrime.gov.in par report karo ya 1930 par call karo.
- OTP, UPI PIN, bank details kabhi share mat karo.
