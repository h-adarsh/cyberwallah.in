# What Is Digital Arrest?

Digital Arrest is a scam where fraudsters pretend to be police, telecom officers, or cyber crime officials and pressure victims to transfer money immediately.

## How the Scam Usually Works

1. You receive a threatening call claiming your number or Aadhaar is involved in a crime.
2. The caller creates urgency and says you are under investigation.
3. They ask you to stay on video call and stop talking to family.
4. They force a "verification payment" to a so-called safe account.

## Red Flags

- Threats of instant arrest over a phone call
- Requests for OTP, PIN, screen sharing, or immediate transfer
- Fake legal language and spoofed caller IDs

## What You Should Do

- Disconnect the call.
- Verify using official helplines or local police channels.
- Report at cybercrime.gov.in or call 1930.
- Never share OTP, UPI PIN, or bank credentials.
